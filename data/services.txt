Build Synergy — Services

Build Synergy is a small web development and design studio based in Cape Town, South Africa. We build custom websites, SaaS MVPs, and brand identities for startups and growing small businesses.

--- What We Build ---

Custom Websites
We design and build marketing sites, portfolio sites, and landing pages from scratch. We do not use WordPress or page builders. Every project is hand-coded using modern frameworks. Typical turnaround: 3–6 weeks.

SaaS MVPs
We take early-stage product ideas and build a working, deployable MVP. This includes frontend, backend, database, authentication, and deployment. We work closely with founders who have validated their idea but need to get something in users' hands. Typical turnaround: 8–14 weeks depending on scope.

Brand Identity
Logo, colour palette, typography system, and a basic brand guide. Often bundled with a website project. Can be done standalone. Typical turnaround: 2–3 weeks.

E-commerce
We build Shopify storefronts with custom themes and integrations. We do not build custom e-commerce from scratch unless it is part of a larger SaaS engagement.

--- Pricing ---

We charge project-based, not hourly.

Custom Website: R18,000 – R45,000 depending on scope and content volume
SaaS MVP: R65,000 – R140,000 depending on feature set
Brand Identity: R8,500 – R16,000
E-commerce (Shopify): R12,000 – R28,000

All prices are in South African Rand and exclude VAT. A 50% deposit is required before work begins. The remaining 50% is due on delivery.

--- What We Do Not Do ---

We do not offer ongoing maintenance retainers at this time.
We do not do print design, social media management, or SEO campaigns.
We do not build mobile apps (native iOS or Android).
We do not work with clients who have no budget defined — every engagement starts with a scoping call.

--- Clients We Work Best With ---

Startups at the seed or pre-seed stage who need a first digital presence or an MVP.
Small businesses that have outgrown a DIY website and want something professional.
Founders who know what they want but need someone to build it properly.

We are not the right fit for large enterprises, government contracts, or clients who need a team of 10 or more developers.
